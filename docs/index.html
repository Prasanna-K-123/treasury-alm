Mortality, curve tail and measurement limits

SSA 2023 period mortality describes the US Social Security area, not Indian annuitants or future cohort improvements. The force transformation is q*=1-(1-q)^m. The maximum paying age is 120; later benefits are zero. Curve values beyond 30 years are extrapolated assumptions. DV01 is a positive magnitude: a small positive parallel rate shift reduces liability PV. Deterministic stresses do not estimate probabilities or solvency capital.
